Voice of the Heart / Mineradio embedded surface

This directory contains the web surface and iOS bridge extracted from the
Voice of the Heart iOS package supplied for integration into Beans Music.
The upstream project is based on Mineradio and is distributed under GPL-3.0.
The corresponding source and license are retained in the project workspace
under .voice-of-the-heart-source.

Original project:
